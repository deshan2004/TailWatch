How It Works

Simple steps to make your community safer for everyone

When you see a stray dog in your area, observe its behavior and health condition from a safe distance.

Report with Details

Use our app to report the dog with location, health status, and photo if possible.

Alert the Community

Your report immediately appears on the community map to warn others and coordinate help.
